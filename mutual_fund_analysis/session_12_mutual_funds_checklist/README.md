## 1. Core Lecture Discussion & Concepts

In this session, Parth Verma systematically deconstructs the foundational and operational mechanics of **The Institutional 6-Point Scheme Selection Checklist**. 

### Primary Themes Addressed by the Instructor:
- **Parth outlines a structured 6-point checklist to filter thousands of mutual fund schemes down to a high-conviction portfolio**:
- **1. Category Mandate Match**: Ensuring the scheme aligns with your personal investment horizon (Equity > 5-7 yrs; Debt < 3 yrs).
- **2. Rolling Return Consistency**: Evaluating 3-year and 5-year rolling returns against the category median and benchmark index over at least one full market cycle (7+ years).
- **3. Risk-Adjusted Metrics Filter**: Seeking Sharpe Ratio higher than category average and Beta between 0.85 - 1.05.
- **4. Downside Capture Ratio (< 85-90%)**: Proving the fund protects capital during market corrections, combined with Upside Capture (> 95%).
- **5. AUM Size Check**: Avoiding schemes with bloated AUM in Small/Mid cap space (liquidity risk) or excessively tiny AUM in Debt space (concentration risk).
- **6. Skin in the Game**: Reviewing SEBI's 'Skin in the Game' disclosures—how much of their personal money does the fund manager and AMC leadership have invested in the scheme.

## 2. Real-World Context & Detailed Scenarios

To translate the lecture discussion into actionable investor understanding, consider the following practical scenarios and industry mechanics:

- Capture Ratio Calculation Example: If Nifty drops 10% and Fund X drops only 7%, Downside Capture = 70%. If Nifty rises 15% and Fund X gains 16%, Upside Capture = 106%. A fund with Downside Capture < 80% and Upside Capture > 100% is an exceptional compounder.
- Small Cap AUM Bloat Trap: Nippon India Small Cap or HDFC Small Cap crossing ₹40,000-50,000 Crores in AUM. At that scale, deploying ₹500 Crores into a ₹3,000 Crore micro-cap company causes massive price distortion and liquidity bottlenecks, forcing the fund to buy large caps or hold excess cash.

## 3. Practical Investor Takeaways

1. A disciplined 6-point checklist prevents emotional chasing of last year's top-performing schemes.
2. Check Downside Capture Ratio to ensure capital protection during bear phases.
