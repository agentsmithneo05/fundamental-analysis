## 1. Core Lecture Discussion & Concepts

In this session, Parth Verma systematically deconstructs the foundational and operational mechanics of **Debt Mutual Funds Architecture & Fixed Income Risk Mechanics**. 

### Primary Themes Addressed by the Instructor:
- **Debt Funds Anatomy**: Lending capital to governments and corporations in exchange for fixed coupon interest and principal repayment.
- **The 16 SEBI Debt Categories based on Duration**: Overnight Funds (1-day maturity), Liquid Funds (up to 91 days), Ultra-Short Duration (3-6 months), Low Duration (6-12 months), Money Market (up to 1 year), Short Duration (1-3 years), Medium Duration (3-4 years), and Long Duration (7+ years).
- **Macaulay Duration & Modified Duration**: Measuring the price sensitivity of a bond fund to changes in central bank (RBI) policy interest rates. When RBI cuts rates, bond prices rise (longer duration funds gain); when RBI hikes rates, bond prices drop.
- **Credit Risk vs Duration Risk**: Credit Risk Funds invest in AA and below-rated corporate papers to earn higher yields (YTM) but carry default risks; Gilt Funds invest solely in Sovereign Government Securities (G-Secs) with zero credit default risk but high interest rate duration volatility.

## 2. Real-World Context & Detailed Scenarios

To translate the lecture discussion into actionable investor understanding, consider the following practical scenarios and industry mechanics:

- The Franklin Templeton Crisis (April 2020) Case Study: Parth discusses the catastrophic freezing of 6 debt schemes (Credit Risk, Short Term Income, Ultra Short Bond) during Covid-19 liquidity shock. The schemes held illiquid lower-rated corporate bonds; when panic redemptions hit, the fund could not sell bonds in secondary markets to raise cash, forcing the AMC to wind up ₹28,000 Crores of assets.
- Duration Sensitivity Math: If a Gilt Fund has a Modified Duration of 7 years and the RBI unexpectedly hikes repo rates by 100 bps (1%), the fund's NAV drops by approximately $7 	imes 1\% = 7\%$. In contrast, a Liquid Fund with 30-day duration experiences virtually zero capital fluctuation.

## 3. Practical Investor Takeaways

1. Debt funds are not risk-free fixed deposits; they carry credit default risk and interest rate duration risk.
2. Match fund duration with your investment horizon: use Overnight/Liquid funds for emergency reserves, Short Duration for 1-3 years, and Gilts for rate-cut cycles.
